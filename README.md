# 🌍 WeatherGlobe — Bangladesh & Global Weather Platform

A modern, fast, multi-language weather application with real-time GPS location tracking, coastal/marine safety advisories, transit delay risk alerts, 50-language support, and audio voice weather narration.

---

## 📱 Mobile App Releases

WeatherGlobe is configured to automatically build and release mobile binaries via GitHub Actions:

- **🤖 Android APK (`WeatherGlobe-Android.apk`)**: Direct installation on Android phones and tablets.
- **🏬 Google Play Store Bundle (`WeatherGlobe-GooglePlay.aab`)**: Official Android App Bundle ready to upload to Google Play Console.
- **🍎 iPhone / iOS**:
  - Open in iOS Safari and tap **Share > Add to Home Screen** for native standalone PWA experience.
  - Or download `WeatherGlobe-iOS-Project.zip` and build in Xcode via Capacitor iOS.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📍 **Auto GPS & City Detection**: Immediate weather metrics for current location worldwide.
- 🔊 **Voice Narration (Audio TTS)**: High-clarity audio summaries for rural and accessibility users.
- 🚢 **Coastal & River Port Marine Advisories**: Signal flags, sea condition warnings, and ferry/launch safety status.
- 🚆 **Transit Delay Risk**: Flight and train schedule delay risk based on rain, fog, and visibility.
- 🌐 **Multi-Language Support**: 50+ languages with full RTL support (Arabic, etc.).
- 💎 **Modern Clean UI**: Designed for maximum readability and speed.
